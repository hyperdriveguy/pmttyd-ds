retains original `enter_gorotsuki_town`, its ship animation children and
`ship_pera` requests. The source REL hash and broader story graph remain in
[harbor source bindings](STORY_HARBOR_OPENING_BINDINGS.md).
[Read-only verifier](../../../tools/verify_opening_harbor_requests.py) pins

Original014/015 retain literal speaker0 and unchanged msg/US control tokens.
The scene now gates their publication on the ship/camera requests, rather
than exposing both messages immediately on arrival. Story3 publishes at the
original main-stream three-second departure wait. The native frontend retains
the still-running ship scene until its eight-second departure ends; handing
that detached source event to ordinary field rendering remains separate.

Private original harbor conversion keeps the full `S_fune_a` and
`S_a_hikinami` subtrees plus their local/world vertex bindings. Only those
source subtrees receive ship transforms; child rudder/wheel/chimney/screw
rotations compose through the original hierarchy. The wake separates its
visibility group from the ship so a ship-only offscreen target cannot capture
wake geometry. Original later-story boats remain hidden in this source story2
branch. The original wake stays at the departure GW11=-475 while GW4 moves.
The source rocking callback retains the2×amplitude / sysMsec2FrameFloat(2000)
step and endpoints; original subevent ordering is represented by the native
single-tick owner, rather than executing the full REL scheduler.

`ship_pera` uses original `a/OFF_d_kaiten`, distinct kind2/kind1 captured faces,
Z_1 then original A_1 frames0..60, with61 retained sparse deformation keys.
The native renderer draws only the actual original ship into a transparent
isolated render target and packs that framebuffer into an owned texture.
Original source capture608×480 and virtual point(0,0,375) have a native400×240
viewport/orthographic outer-projection adapter. No fallback original TPL or
invented raster is substituted for the capture. A_1 final-pose evaluation and
actual rendered/synchronized frame acknowledgment both precede restoring the
source map object and yaw0. Capture or required asset failure does not count
as successful presentation. GPU/texture resources are separate from book
captures and released with frontend scene ownership.

Original arbitrary offscreen projection, full GX image/capture services,
material callback alpha, environmental/MusyX/spatial sound owners, wake
playrate, original camera lenses and runtime scheduler equivalence remain
separate incomplete services. Native raster adaptation and hardware fidelity
are not asserted by these host checks. The native dummy jump now binds the recovered used `evt_npc_jump_position_nohit`

Its source coefficient at8041fdfc is -4.0. Original integer arguments are
X0, Y=evtGetValue(GW8), Z0, duration1800ms and height100. Retained dummy Y
publishes through Q1024 before its GW8 consumer. Y uses the original nested
fused arithmetic `fma(t,fma(-4*height,t,(targetY-startY)-coefficient),startY)`.
At equality1800ms it snaps the integer target and restores the saved0x80000

a collision-aware jump or substitute sine arc. The current explicit native
fresh bank supplies initial GW8=0; source opaque-provider writes and the whole

source census is integrated.

Native `OpeningSequence` requires actual scene admission after maps/backdrops,
actors and image resources load and draw. Failed native geometry/image/capture
records an error and stops story progression; it cannot produce a successful
arrival save. Rendering publishes admission/failure and final-pose acknowledgments,
while simulation owns all motion clocks. Per-object inverse/rotation terms and
dense child bindings cache outside the per-vertex hot path; no original
geometry or art was replaced to obtain that improvement.
